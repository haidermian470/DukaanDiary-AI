## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for micro-to-small retail merchants, kirayana owners, and dukandaars in fast-paced commercial environments. The brand personality conveys absolute financial reliability, crisp ledger clarity, and accessible intelligence. It bridges traditional handwritten bahi-khata workflows with automated computer-vision ledgering.

### Design Movement & Core Principles
- **Modern Utility Fintech:** A blend of ultra-clean corporate fintech discipline and localized physical shop utility. It rejects noisy decorations in favor of hyper-legible figures, decisive status coloring, and high-contrast surfaces operable under harsh ambient sunlight.
- **Tactile Trust:** Financial records must feel immutable yet effortless. High-touch interactive targets, unambiguous numeric states, and deliberate badge hierarchy assure the shopkeeper that every rupee (PKR) is accounted for.
- **Bilingual Contextual Clarity:** Interfaces seamlessly balance primary English technical terms with universal Roman-Urdu and Urdu business concepts (*Rokad*, *Udhaar*, *Khata*, *Tasdeeq*) without visual clatter.
- **Differentiated AI Presence:** Automated OCR receipt audits, voice ledgering, and payment screenshot verifications are styled with a dedicated intelligence aura (Indigo/Violet) so merchants can immediately distinguish between verified cash reality and algorithmic suggestions.

## Colors

The palette uses functional semantic assignment tailored to mercantile accounting. Because financial apps for retail handle rapid cash flow assessments, color carries strict operational meaning rather than purely ornamental value.

### Color Roles & Semantics
- **Primary (Emerald / Cash Inflow / Verified):** `#047857` (darkened for AA contrast on light grounds), supported by `#10B981` and background tint `#ECFDF5`. Applied to incoming payments, verified bank screenshots (Tasdeeq Shuda), net positive balances, and primary confirmations.
- **Secondary (Indigo / AI Intelligence & Automation):** `#4F46E5`, supported by `#6366F1` and background tint `#EEF2FF`. Reserved strictly for OCR evidence scanning, automated screenshot validation, audio ledger dictation, and predictive cash forecasts.
- **Tertiary (Crimson / Outflow / Urgent Udhaar):** `#DC2626`, supported by `#EF4444` and soft tint `#FEF2F2`. Signifies outgoing inventory costs, overdue credit, high-risk merchant debt, and manual deletions.
- **Warning / Review (Amber / Pending Khata):** `#D97706`, supported by `#F59E0B` and soft tint `#FFFBEB`. Identifies unconfirmed customer transfers, pending reconciliation, and grace-period receivables.
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces:** 
  - Base canvas: `#F8F9FA`
  - Elevated card surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Secondary group surface: `#F1F5F9`
  - Structural stroke/borders: `#E2E8F0`
  - Subtle separators: `#F1F5F9`
- **Text & Ink:**
  - Primary text: `#0F172A` (deep slate navy, high readability)
  - Secondary text: `#334155`
  - Tertiary/Muted meta: `#64748B`
  - Inverted ink: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels. Its open apertures, geometric precision, and distinct numeral forms ensure that transaction rows, receipt IDs, and decimal points remain immediately readable during high-speed counter operations.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Financial Formatting
- **Currency & Numerics:** Currency amounts must consistently use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to ensure exact vertical decimal alignment in transaction ledgers.
- **PKR Token Pairing:** The currency denomination `Rs.` or `PKR` is placed as a preceding label token (`label-md` or `label-sm`), styled in secondary slate (`#334155`), while the amount itself scales according to `currency-display` or `currency-card`.
- **Bilingual Subtext:** Primary transaction names are styled in `title-md` (`#0F172A`). Accompanying Urdu/Roman-Urdu operational descriptors (such as *"Udhaar Wasool"* or *"Naqd Rokad"*) are set beneath in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first around rapid single-thumb navigation, catering to one-handed phone use behind counter glass and crowded store checkouts.

### Structural Flow
- **Mobile Container:** Full fluid width bounded with a baseline outer margin of `1rem` (16px). Cards and actionable items snap to edge-to-edge margins to maximize tap real estate.
- **Tablet / Counter POS:** Shifts to a 6-to-8 column layout with a fixed summary rail on the right for continuous active cart or khata balances.
- **Desktop:** Centered 12-column grid maxed at 1120px to prevent horizontal scanning fatigue for comprehensive monthly tax and inventory audit views.
- **Touch Target Integrity:** Every clickable card, entry point, voice prompt, or WhatsApp share button retains a strict minimum tap target of 48px height, padded with `space-md` horizontally.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses a restrained, crisp depth model combining structural 1px border framing with soft ambient surface diffusion. Heavy drop shadows are omitted to maintain an uncluttered ledger appearance.

### Depth Tiers
- **Flat Surface (`level-0`):** `#F8F9FA` background. Borderless.
- **Resting Ledger Card (`level-1`):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive Active/Hover Item (`level-2`):** Pure white `#FFFFFF`, border `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Modals & Quick Action Trays (`level-3`):** Bottom sheets for receipt upload, numeric payment keypads, and voice memos. Border `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **AI Verification Scrim (`level-glow`):** During OCR or payment evidence scanning, cards employ a subtle dual layer: an inner glow `0 0 0 1.5px #6366F1` plus a soft diffuse bloom `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined modern curvature (`roundedness: 2` with standard radii scaling between 8px and 16px). This creates friendly, approachable containers without feeling cartoonish or wasting dense data screen real estate.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** 16px (`rounded-lg` equivalent in this system) border radius. Gives ledger entries a distinct, self-contained modular identity.
- **Inner Micro-Surfaces & Input Fields:** 10px to 12px border radius. Balances visual softness with compact data entry.
- **Buttons & Semantic Badges:** 8px to 10px for standard utility buttons; full pills (`9999px`) for contextual statuses (*Tasdeeq Shuda*, *Udhaar*, *Rokad*), filters, and quick chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Confirm (Emerald):** Background `#047857`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius 10px, min-height 48px. Active press: `#065F46`.
- **AI Action / Scan Evidence (Indigo):** Background `#4F46E5`, text `#FFFFFF`, paired with a secondary voice/camera icon.
- **Destructive / Settle Udhaar (Red):** Background `#FEF2F2`, border 1px solid `#FCA5A5`, text `#DC2626`.
- **Secondary / WhatsApp Invoice:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Verified Payment (Tasdeeq):** Tint `#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`, paired with a solid mini checkmark icon.
- **Credit Due (Udhaar):** Tint `#FEF2F2`, border `#FECACA`, text `#B91C1C`.
- **Pending Verification (Zer-e-Ghaur):** Tint `#FFFBEB`, border `#FDE68A`, text `#92400E`.
- **AI Smart Extract:** Tint `#EEF2FF`, border `#C7D2FE`, text `#4338CA`, accompanied by a spark icon.

### Form Inputs & Numeric Keypads
- **Amount Input:** Prominent left-anchored `Rs.` currency prefix in `#475569`. Text uses `currency-display` (32px bold). Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1`. Focus state: border `#047857` with 3px shadow ring `#ECFDF5`.
- **Khata Search & Contact Picker:** Auto-suggest input with rapid Pakistani contact formatting (`+92 3XX XXXXXXX`), voice-search micro button pinned to the trailing edge.

### Ledger List Cards
- **Structure:** 3-column flex layout.
  - *Left:* Visual semantic pill or contact avatar with initials.
  - *Center:* Customer/Vendor name (`title-md`), subline showing timestamp and category badge or Roman-Urdu tags (`body-sm`).
  - *Right:* Financial net amount. Inflow (`+ Rs.`) in `#047857`, Outflow (`- Rs.`) in `#DC2626`. Explicit indicator icon (arrow down-left vs. arrow up-right).

### Specialized Product Components
- **Payment Evidence Matcher Card:** Split preview showing customer-submitted screenshot (Easypaisa, JazzCash, 1Link) beside AI-extracted fields (Transaction ID, Amount, Timestamp). Visual confidence meter labeled "Tasdeeq Score".
- **Daily Rokad Counter (Cash-in-Hand Ribbon):** Top-anchored summary banner displaying opening cash, net day sales, remaining customer credit, and net balance in 3 clear partitioned columns.
